We have that
$$
\begin{align*}
\begin{pmatrix}
a_{n+2} \\
a_{n+1}
\end{pmatrix}&=\begin{pmatrix}
-4 & -7 \\
1 & 0
\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}
a_{n+1} \\
a_{n}
\end{pmatrix},
\tag*{}\end{align*}
$$
so
$$
\begin{align*}
\begin{pmatrix}
a_{n+1} \\
a_{n}
\end{pmatrix}&=\begin{pmatrix}
-4 & -7 \\
1 & 0
\end{pmatrix}^{n}\begin{pmatrix}
a_{1} \\
a_{0}
\end{pmatrix}\\
&=\begin{pmatrix}
-4 & -7 \\
1 & 0
\end{pmatrix}^{n}\begin{pmatrix}
-4  \\
1 
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag*{}\end{align*}
$$
We have that $a_{2}=9$, so
$$
\begin{align*}
\begin{pmatrix}
a_{n+1} & a_{n+2} \\
a_{n} & a_{n+1}
\end{pmatrix}&=\begin{pmatrix}
-4 & -7 \\
1 & 0
\end{pmatrix}^{n}\begin{pmatrix}
-4 & 9 \\
1 & -4
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag*{}\end{align*}
$$
Taking determinants on both sides, we get
$$
\begin{align*}
a_{n+1}^{2}-a_{n}a_{n+2}&=\begin{vmatrix}
-4 & -7 \\
1 & 0
\end{vmatrix}^{n}\cdot \begin{vmatrix}
-4 & 9 \\
1 & -4
\end{vmatrix}\\
&=7^{n+1}.
\tag*{}\end{align*}
$$
Thus we have that
$$
\begin{align*}
a_{50}^{2}-a_{49}a_{51}&=7^{50},
\tag*{}\end{align*}
$$
which has $51$ positive integer divisors consisting of the powers of $7$ from $7^{0}$ to $7^{50}$.
